Approach

How I take a feature from idea to live

The process I follow for every new feature.

  1. Stage 1

    Specify

    Write the requirements, API specification and acceptance criteria.

  2. Stage 2

    Prototype

    Mock up the screens in Lovable before engineering time is spent.

  3. Stage 3

    Test

    A/B test the screens with the people who will use them.

  4. Stage 4

    Build

    Build it myself, or lead the team that builds it, through to production.

Judgement

How I make product calls

Six principles, each taken from a real decision on this page.

Prove demand before building

PrepPO launched free with no signup. Payments stay specified but unbuilt until real users validate the idea.

Seen in PrepPO

Prioritise by value, not volume

Every stakeholder wanted their category first. I chose IT, telecoms and professional services because they carry the most contract value.

Seen in contract analysis

Listen to users, let evidence decide

The engagement team votes on tickets. Votes shape priorities and roadmap signals without overriding the formal score.

Seen in the Backlog Hub

Use rules when trust matters

Supplier matching uses exact rules, not AI. Only confirmed matches are shown, because a wrong match costs more than a missing one.

Seen in Supplier Framework

Ask for the evidence

A feature only scores high on roadmap alignment with a named source, and every supplier listing links to where it came from.

Seen in the Backlog Hub

Remove bottlenecks, including me

Standard reports became self serve, and the Toolkit moved to Cloudflare so other Product Owners don't need me to run it.

Seen in Market Intelligence
